\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
\vspace{-0.2em}
We presented SOMA, an efficient multi-turn LLM serving framework that uses early dialogue turns to adapt a small surrogate to the original model's session-local behavior. It mines soft-prompt directions, distills them with LoRA, and switches only when alignment is sufficient. Experiments show that SOMA better preserves original-model responses, improves task-grounded quality, and reduces serving cost once warm-start overhead is amortized. These results suggest that session-local surrogate serving is a practical direction for efficient multi-turn LLM deployment \rev{when sessions are long and locally coherent enough to amortize the warm-start cost, as Figure~\ref{fig:operating}(a) quantifies}. \rev{Limitations and future work are discussed in Appendix~\ref{sec:limitations}.}
