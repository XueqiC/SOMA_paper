\subsection*{Ethics statement}
This work uses only publicly available benchmarks under their licenses and involves no human subjects or new personal data. Replacing a large model with a smaller surrogate can degrade response quality when a conversation drifts, especially in high-stakes domains; SOMA's acceptance gate and rollback reduce but do not remove this risk, so deployments should keep monitoring, conservative thresholds, and privacy-preserving handling of dialogue history (Appendices~\ref{sec:broader_impact} and~\ref{sec:limitations}).

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
An anonymized implementation of SOMA with scripts for the main experiments is available at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/SOMA-4175}. Appendix~\ref{sec:app_dataset} and the following subsections describe the datasets, filtering, baselines, hyperparameters, judge protocol, and software versions, and Appendix~\ref{sec:app_proof} states the assumptions and proofs of all theoretical results.
