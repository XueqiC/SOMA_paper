\subsection*{AI use statement}
We used generative AI tools to polish the writing and to help with parts of the evaluation and plotting code. The research itself is the authors' own work, and the authors checked all AI-assisted text and code and take full responsibility for this paper.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}
This work uses public benchmarks under their licenses and involves no human subjects or new personal data. A smaller surrogate may lower response quality when a conversation drifts, especially in high-stakes domains, so deployments should keep monitoring, conservative thresholds, and privacy-preserving handling of dialogue history (Appendices~\ref{sec:broader_impact} and~\ref{sec:limitations}).

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
An anonymized implementation of SOMA with scripts for the main experiments is available at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/SOMA-4175}. Appendix~\ref{sec:app_dataset} and the following subsections describe the datasets, filtering, baselines, hyperparameters, judge protocol, and software versions, and Appendix~\ref{sec:app_proof} states the assumptions and proofs of all theoretical results.
